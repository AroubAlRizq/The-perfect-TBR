Density

Sentence length and variation, subordination, and how much of the page is dialogue, combined into one number. It says how much work the prose asks of you — not whether it is any good. Plain is a preference, not a failing.

Stock phrases

How often the writing reaches for a phrase that has been used a thousand times. Breath hitching, jaws ticking, stomachs dropping. This is the measurement that catches what a Wattpad label was only ever guessing at.

Narration

Person and tense, read from the narration with dialogue stripped out first. First-person present divides readers far more sharply than first person alone, so both are reported.

Bring your history
